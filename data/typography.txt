# Heed Cafe - Typography System

## Font Selection
- Arabic: Tajawal / Reem Kufi
- English: Montserrat / Inter

## Font Weights Used
- Light: 300
- Regular: 400
- Medium: 500
- Bold: 700
- Extra Bold: 800

## Typography Hierarchy
- Logo: Reem Kufi (700-800)
- H1: Tajawal (800)
- H2: Tajawal (700)
- H3: Tajawal (600)
- Body: Tajawal (400)
- Caption: Tajawal (300)

## Arabic Typography Style
- Modern Naskh-inspired
- Clean and readable
- Warm character

## English Typography Style
- Geometric Sans-Serif
- Clean lines
- Modern feel

## Web Fonts (Google Fonts)
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Tajawal:wght@200;300;400;500;700;800;900&family=Reem+Kufi:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap');

## Usage Guidelines
- Minimum body text: 16px
- Line height: 1.6-1.8
- Letter spacing: Normal to slightly wide
- Paragraph spacing: 1.5rem
