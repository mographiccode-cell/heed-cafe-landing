# Heed Cafe - Color Palette
# Extracted from Brand Identity Project by Majd Kamal

## Primary Colors
- Sandy Beige: #D4C5A9
- Warm Brown: #8B7355
- Deep Charcoal: #2C2C2C
- Off-White: #F5F0E8

## Secondary Colors
- Desert Terracotta: #C4956A
- Sand Gold: #C9B896
- Earth Brown: #6B5344
- Cream: #F2EBE0

## Accent Colors
- Muted Olive: #7A7A5A
- Warm Gray: #9E9589
- Desert Rose: #B89B8A

## Background Colors
- Primary BG: #F5F0E8 (Off-White)
- Dark BG: #0a0806
- Card BG: #1a1510

## Text Colors
- Primary Text: #2C2C2C
- Light Text: #F5F0E8
- Muted Text: #9E9589

## Color Usage Guidelines
- Background: Off-White (#F5F0E8) for light theme
- Headings: Warm Brown (#8B7355)
- Body Text: Deep Charcoal (#2C2C2C)
- Accents/CTAs: Desert Terracotta (#C4956A)
- Cards: Sandy Beige (#D4C5A9)

## Color Psychology
- Sandy Beige: Warmth, comfort, desert landscape
- Brown: Earthiness, coffee, stability
- Off-White: Clean, modern, spacious
- Terracotta: Heritage, craftsmanship, warmth
