## Brand Description (AR)
هيد ليس مجرد مكان تشرب فيه القهوة — إنه مبنى على الأصالة والانتماء والمجتمع الحقيقي. كل تفصيل في الهوية تم صنعه بقصد، من الألوان المستوحاة من الصحراء إلى الأنماط التي تتردد صداها التراث.

## Brand Description (EN)
Heed is more than a place to drink coffee — it's a space built on authenticity, belonging, and genuine community. Every detail in the brand was crafted with intention from desert inspired colors to patterns that echo heritage.

## Brand Story
A coffee shop and creative space in Jeddah inspired by the timeless spirit of Bedouin life. Heed is more than a place to drink coffee — it's a space built on authenticity, community, and quiet belonging.
